Getting started

What does Resonance do?

When you breathe in, your heart beats a little faster; when you breathe out, it slows down. Resonance guides you to breathe slowly so this wave becomes big and smooth, and shows you live how well it works.

Do I need a chest strap?

No. The Apple Watch works on its own. A Bluetooth chest strap measures every single heartbeat and gives the most precise feedback, especially at faster breathing paces and for HRV numbers like RMSSD.

Which chest straps work?

Any strap that uses the standard Bluetooth heart-rate profile and sends beat-to-beat (RR) intervals, for example the Polar H10 or Garmin HRM series. Straps that only send heart rate also work, just less precisely.

Chest strap problems

My strap doesn't show up
  • Wet the electrodes and put the strap on; most straps only wake up when they touch skin.
  • Don't pair it in iOS Settings. Open Resonance → sensor row → Search for straps.
  • Some straps allow only a few Bluetooth connections. Disconnect it from other apps or devices.
  • Make sure Bluetooth is allowed for Resonance in Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.
The curve looks jumpy or the check-up "didn't count"

That usually means a weak signal. Wet the electrodes, tighten the strap a little and sit still. A Watch band should sit snugly above the wrist bone.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

The Watch app doesn't appear

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and install Resonance.

Sessions aren't saved to Apple Health

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Resonance and turn the categories on. Then in Resonance tap Settings → Reconnect Apple Health.

Why is my HRV in Resonance different from Apple Health?

They're measured in different situations. Apple Health takes short readings at rest, mostly at night. Resonance measures while you breathe slowly, which makes your heart rate swing much more. Compare each number only with itself.

Resonance Plus

What's free and what's in Plus?

Breathing sessions, live feedback, the check-up, the resonance finder, the Apple Watch and Apple Health are free. Plus unlocks your progress and its interpretation: Journey (start to now, trends, what affects you, milestones), the PDF report, Today and age comparisons.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Resonance → Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Resonance Plus → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Plus until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in Apple Health if you allow it. Resonance has no account and no servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes everything Resonance stored. Sessions saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app (Browse → a category → Show All Data, or your profile → Apps → Resonance).

Important

Resonance is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is a relaxation and training tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a heart condition, are pregnant or feel dizzy while breathing slowly, stop and talk to your doctor.
