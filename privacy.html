Privacy Policy

Resonance is built so that your data stays with you. Last updated: 7 October 2026.

The short version
  • Your sessions, notes and settings are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Resonance has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Apple Health data is read and saved only with your permission and never leaves your devices through us.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

2. Data stored on your devices

Resonance stores the following only in the app's private storage on your iPhone and Apple Watch:

  • Your breathing sessions and check-ups: heart-rate readings, beat-to-beat intervals, scores and results.
  • Day tags and notes you add (for example "poor sleep" or "long walk").
  • Your settings, your breathing pace and the name of your paired chest strap.

We have no access to this data. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. If your device backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, it may be included in that backup, which Apple or your computer manages.

3. Apple Health

If you allow it, Resonance uses Apple Health as follows:

  • Reads: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, heartbeat series, workouts, mindful minutes, date of birth and biological sex. Age and sex are used only to compare your values with published reference values for your age group.
  • Saves: each session as a Mind & Body workout and Mindful Minutes, the heart rate and (on Apple Watch) active energy and (with a chest strap) the heartbeat series measured during it, optionally the session's heart rate variability, and optionally how you felt (State of Mind).

Health data is processed only on your devices. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Resonance.

4. Apple Watch

Settings and session results are exchanged between your iPhone and your Apple Watch over Apple's encrypted connection between your own devices. Nothing passes through our servers, because there are none.

5. Bluetooth chest strap

Resonance connects directly to a Bluetooth heart-rate strap you choose and receives only heart-rate and heartbeat data from it. The app does not use your location.

6. Purchases

Resonance Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether Plus is active. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

7. Notifications and reports

The optional morning reminder is scheduled on your iPhone; no notification service of ours is involved. The PDF report is created on your iPhone and is shared only if you choose to share it yourself.

8. Crash reports

If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and keep them secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Because Resonance does not send your data to us, we usually hold no personal data about you; you control everything directly on your devices and in the Health app. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to answer it and delete it when it's no longer needed. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Age

Resonance is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Its reference values come from studies of adults. Please don't use Resonance if you are under 18.

12. Changes

If the way Resonance handles data changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Resonance is a relaxation and training tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
